# Daurade 1.0.1

Every key plays one note. [Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca)'s own keyboard was still on next to Daurade's: each key also played its raw note on the
first track and out on MIDI, so a key off the scale sounded a semitone clash. It now stays silent.

Runs on the FM-1. To go back: Felucca's or [Tuna](https://github.com/delirioux/tuna)'s installer, or the official V15.

# Daurade 1.0

- MENU (hold HOME) shows the CPU, the audio's load, as Tuna does.
- Newer FM-1 units with a UC25HQ80 flash save their settings and sets again (Felucca's fix for its issue #320):
  0.1 did not recognise that flash and ran without saving.

## Daurade 0.1

The first release.

- Ten styles on two sides: CHROME (FILTER HOUSE, ROBOT POP, SHRED, NIGHT DRIVE, SLOW JAM) and SESSION (DISCO,
  SEQUENCER, FUNK, BALLAD, FINALE); a song for every ALGORITHM number; style and song change on the bar line.
- SWEEP, PUMP, VOICE and ENERGY on the four knobs; DROP on REC (tapped or held), SOLO on SEQ.
- The keys play the lead, snapped to the scale; their LEDs show the chord.
- Forty sounds on Felucca's engines, levels evened out; a robot on the VOICE engine, singing a vowel phrase in
  SLOW JAM (PHRASE).
- A very crisp drum kit of Daurade's own (CC0) with deep kicks, and Felucca's DRUM engine kits.
- The arc: intro, main, build, drop, solo, breakdown.
- Eight sets; settings saved while stopped; MIDI out on channels 1, 2, 3 and 10.
- The NEBULA palette, by default.
- In the browser: the same firmware on Felucca's emulator.

Runs on the FM-1. To go back: Felucca's or Tuna's installer, or the official V15.
