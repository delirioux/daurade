DRUMS: Daurade's drum kit, seven one-shots (00_kick_punch, 01_kick_round, 02_snare, 03_clap, 04_hat, 05_open_hat,
06_crash), synthesized by tools/da_drums.py (seeded, so it always makes the same files): no recording and no
third-party sample is in them. 16-bit mono WAV at 44.1 kHz; tools/gen_samples.py encodes them into the firmware.
